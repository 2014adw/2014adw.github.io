Privacy Policy

App: ComfyCar Backup
Last updated: September 22, 2026

What this app does

ComfyCar Backup is a personal, single-user backup tool. It copies files from the owner's private server into the owner's own Google Drive account, into a dedicated Backup/ folder. The app is used only by the owner of the Google account it is connected to.

Data access and use

The app requests the drive scope in order to upload backup copies and manage backup rotation (deleting old backup snapshots inside the Backup/ folder). The app does not read, copy, modify or delete any Google user data outside this stated purpose.

Data sharing

No data is shared with any third party. All backup copies remain inside the owner's Google Drive account. The app itself runs on infrastructure controlled by the owner.

Data retention

Backup snapshots are retained according to a rotation policy configured by the owner (currently: 3 monthly snapshots, 26 weekly snapshots). Older snapshots are deleted automatically by the backup software, and the owner may delete all backups at any time by removing the Backup/ folder from Google Drive and revoking the app's access.
